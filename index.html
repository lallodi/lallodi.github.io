I am an Associate Professor and head of the Threat Analysis group in the Security Cluster of the Eindhoven University of Technology, the Netherlands. We study attacker actions to help analysts find their traces in log and alert data. Our two research lines are cyber threat intelligence (the cybercriminal ecosystem and social engineering) and security operations (analyst processes, analyst training, and detection rule engineering in SOCs). Our work draws from computer security, economics, risk analysis, and criminology.

Research infrastructure

My group runs two research infrastructures that also deliver services in practice.

Security monitoring

ESH-SOC

Eindhoven Security Hub Security Operations Center
Professional services
  • Managed detection for organisations in industry and government
  • 24/7 monitoring of logs and network traffic
  • More than 140 detection use cases covering all 14 MITRE ATT&CK tactics
  • Response to the most severe incidents, with a report in under an hour
  • Incident, monthly KPI, and identity reports for auditors

Managed by my group; I am its Scientific Director. Initiated by TU/e with Fontys and KEMBIT. We carry out our security operations research in and with the SOC.

eshsoc.nl · ESH-SOC at the Security Cluster

Threat intelligence

CTILab

Cyber threat intelligence lab
Professional services
  • Access to our cyber threat intelligence for third parties
  • Indicators and detection rules for the ESH-SOC
  • Analyses of criminal markets and communities

Run with the Jheronimus Academy of Data Science (JADS). The lab collects data from underground forums and Telegram communities with crawlers and classification pipelines such as TeleHunt.
